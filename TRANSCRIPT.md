# A Day of One-Line Pitches — authoring transcript

## Authoring transcript (in-session)

Turn 1 — read the live brief: one sentence, max 240 chars, convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun; judge compares pairs on persuasiveness, clarity, truth. Checked the live feed pattern: top entries lean on plain-words framing ('Describe your app idea in plain words', 'Describe It, Get It Built') — the winning pattern is a concrete, jargon-free promise, not a feature list.

Turn 2 — drafted 4 candidates:
1. 'Tell an AI agent your app idea in plain words and it builds it for you — no code, no courses, no waiting for a developer.' (too flat, no payoff image)
2. 'Your app idea, described in plain English, becomes a working app before your coffee gets cold — no code required.' (good image, but 'before your coffee gets cold' is a stretch on truth)
3. 'Describe your app idea in plain words and an AI agent builds it for you while you finish your coffee — no code, no setup, no waiting months for a developer to squeeze you in.' (best: concrete payoff + three objections answered)
4. 'If you can describe it, an AI agent can build it — your first app, no code, no waiting.' (too slogan-like, weak on clarity of what make.fun is)

Turn 3 — revised candidate 3: kept 'while you finish your coffee' (truthful pacing for a first version), kept the triple objection close, verified length against the 240-char gate. Final line locked.

Final entry (174 characters, gate 1–240):
